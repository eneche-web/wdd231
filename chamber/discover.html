About the Area

Abuja Municipal Area Council

Abuja Municipal Area Council, also known as AMAC, contains many of the central districts and important landmarks of Nigeria's Federal Capital Territory. The area combines government institutions, businesses, residentail communities, shopping districts, recreation, culture, and tourism.

Abuja officially became the nation's capital in 1991 as planned. Today, the city continues to attract businesses, residents, visitors, and organizations from across Nigeria and all over the world.
