Website Planning Document

This site plan defines the purpose, target audience, content, design, navigatioin and functionality of the Abuja Municipal Area Council Chamber Website

Site Name

Abuja Municipal Area Council Chamber

The name represents a business organization fashioned to connect, support, and promote businesses across the Abuja Municipal Area Council, FCT Abuja, Nigeria,

Site Purpose

The Abuja Municipal Area Council Chember websute is expected to provide information about local business and help connect business owners, residents, visitors, and potential investors. The website would provide a business directory, events, membership information, and information about the Chember.

The website is envisaged to encourage local business networking, promote ecpnomic activities, and provide a central online resource for businesses operating in Abuja.

Target Audience

  • Local business owners and enterpreneurs
  • Residents looking for local businesses and services
  • Visitors interested in businesses in Abuja
  • Pontential investors and business partners
  • Professionals and organizations seeking local connections

Responsive Design

The website will be design with the expectation to work from 320 pixels wide through large desktop screens without horizontal scrolling. The navigation directory cards, images, text, and other content is expected to adjust according to the available screen size.

Accessibility and SEO

  • Semantic HTML element will be used.
  • Images will have descriptive alt text.
  • Navigation will be keyboard accessible.
  • Buttons and links will have clear purpose.
  • Color contrast will be checked.
  • Pages will include descriptive titles and meta description.
  • The layout will be responsive.
  • JavaScript errors will be checked using DevTools
  • Lighthouse will be used to test accessibility, SEO, and best practices.
