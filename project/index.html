WELCOME TO BUILDWISE

Learn before you build.

Construction materials play an important role in the delivery, safety, appearance, durability, and cost of a building. As such, understanding the many materials available can sometimes be difficult.

BuildWise brings useful material information together in one simple place. wheter you are a student, homeowner, apprentice, or someone interested in construction, you can use this guide to learn the besic.

WHY BUILDWISE?

Simple information for better understanding.

You dont need to be a professional engineer to learn the basic of construction materials. BuildWise presents information in a straightforward way so that beginners can understand the purpose of different materials.

  • Easy to understand Materials information is written in simple language.
  • Easy to search Finding materials by name or category
  • Useful comparisions Review properties, uses, advantages, and considerations.
